The town council reviewed its annual budget at Tuesday's meeting. Total spending is planned at 4.2 million dollars, up 6 percent from last year. Road repairs take the largest share at 1.3 million, followed by schools at 1.1 million and parks at 450,000. The council expects property taxes to cover about 70 percent of the budget, with state grants and parking fees making up the rest. Two members objected to a 15 percent increase in the parks budget, arguing that the money should go to repairing the old library roof instead. A final vote is scheduled for next month, after a public hearing where residents can comment on the plan.
